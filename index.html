The Summons

He was cast down from glory, but he never forgot the law. Now he prosecutes day and night. He studies your patterns, catalogs your agreements, and files charges before the Judge for every door you opened and never closed. He does not need to make you fall.

He only needs you to agree.

Told entirely from the perspective of the Accuser himself, a cold and meticulous prosecutor who calls himself the Legalist, this gripping theological allegory pulls back the veil on the unseen legal war being waged over every human soul. Step inside his case files and witness the courtroom of Heaven through the eyes of the prosecution.

Every case looks airtight. Until the Advocate stands up. And the blood speaks a better word.

Iesha D. Golden

Iesha D. Golden is a board-certified Nurse Practitioner with over a decade of healthcare experience, including a background in critical care. Her time in the ICU, where she managed high-stakes crises and responded to urgent conditions, deeply shaped the way she understands both physical and spiritual battles.

Now based in Texas, her writing reflects a diagnostic passion for identifying hidden root causes, confronting spiritual deception, and helping wounded souls find restoration through Jesus Christ. Blending medical precision with a passion for spiritual warfare, Iesha challenges readers to recognize the Accuser's legal tactics, examine their own spiritual patient history, and run boldly to their ultimate Advocate.
