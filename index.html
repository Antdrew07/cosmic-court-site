The author

Iesha
D. Golden

Iesha D. Golden is a board-certified Nurse Practitioner with over a decade of healthcare experience, including a background in critical care. Her time in the ICU—where she managed high-stakes crises and responded to urgent conditions—deeply shaped the way she understands both physical and spiritual battles.

Now based in Texas, her writing reflects a diagnostic passion for identifying hidden root causes, confronting spiritual deception, and helping wounded souls find restoration through Jesus Christ. Blending medical precision with a passion for spiritual warfare, Iesha challenges readers to recognize the Accuser's legal tactics and run boldly to their ultimate Advocate.
